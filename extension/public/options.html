Connect to unfold

Paste your unfold address and a token, and the extension can save pages to your library.

In unfold, open Settings, then Chrome extension, then Create token. Chrome will ask to let the extension talk to this address only.

Ways to save

  • Click the unfold icon, or press Ctrl+Shift+S (⌘+Shift+S on Mac).
  • Right-click a page to save it, or a link to save where it points.
  • Highlight a passage, then right-click to save the page with that passage as your note.
